# Phase 9X — pre-implementation storyboard

The source exports, topology and route/formation engine stay untouched. Scene associations below are editorial topic choices, not additions to graph relationships or content-export mappings.

## A. Home / Current Focus

- Initial frame: existing typed approved headline and discoverable universe. A single Current focus entrance follows the approved themes/supporting line.
- Four scroll states: Flood Accessibility → Geospatial Analysis → AI & Technology → Public Speaking. Titles are exact graph labels; each receives its existing focus statement verbatim.
- Typography: opening left-aligned title, right-aligned spatial title, wide centered distributed title, then lower-left convergent title. Associated graph vocabulary is a scene ingredient, never an echo of the heading.
- Geometry: one persistent projection of real public nodes/edges expands to a network, aligns into analytical rows, distributes across the stage and converges. No links are invented. Canonical interactive D3 graph remains independently available above the film.
- Transition: overlapping scrubbable title masks and 100–300px lateral/vertical moves; geometry and virtual-camera coordinates interpolate continuously. Prose has a long readable hold and shorter cross-over.
- Final frame: convergent communication geometry pulls back; an ordinary link reconnects to the universe. Semantic progress links can seek any scene.

## B. Flood

- Initial frame: canonical formation and approved introductory copy, then a persistent narrative stage.
- Eight scroll states retain all approved sections in order. Question opens a network; Motivation expands it; Method aligns it; Assumptions separates conditional presentation; Exploration distributes; Limitations contracts/dims; Validation shows incomplete visual emphasis; Uncertainty separates bounded positions.
- Typography: substantial alternating composition, with long body copy constrained to a readable column. Background words come from other approved section headings or connected labels, never the active title again.
- Geometry: same seven actual nodes and approved edges in every state; changes are spatial/emphasis metaphors, explicitly not geographic measurements or results.
- Transition/final: continuous position/opacity interpolation with no empty reset. Late uncertainty pulls the camera back and ends at native navigation.

## C. Representative cluster — Projects

- Initial frame: public project constellation and a concise route introduction, without repeated ghost/header/inspector titles.
- States: Nothipotro construction/emergence, then Knowledge Workflow Experiments distributed systems. Exact summaries/statuses and native subject links remain.
- Typography/geometry: an opening left title with right assembly becomes a centered title with a wide graph. Audience-first Design / Knowledge Management supply associated terms through existing public edges.
- Transition/final: the same projection rearranges; a large term reveals through a mask while title and copy swap sides. Subject links preserve native route access; star selection preserves cinematic formation.

## D. Mobile Current Focus

- Initial frame: one Current focus entrance and four compact semantic progress links.
- States: four semantic sections in normal flow, approximately a viewport each but allowed to grow with text. One copy of each statement, no pinned panel or hidden article clone.
- Typography/geometry: alternating large topic alignment, compact real-relationship projection, varying graph geometry above/beside body; no repeated Current focus/status headings. Associated terms are bounded and clipped.
- Transition: small-screen scrub shifts typography 40–100px and transforms the scene diagram; readable prose remains fully present. Reduced motion keeps these distinct authored layouts with no scrubbing.
- Final frame: ordinary navigation; no scroll trap, snap enforcement or horizontal scrolling.

## Shared implementation guardrails

Progressive enhancement only: default semantic sections are readable without JS. Desktop enhancement uses CSS sticky plus one route-scoped GSAP/ScrollTrigger owner, not pin spacers or body scroll locks. Inactive visual panels retain screen-reader prose; their route links are removed from keyboard tab order until the scene is active. Progress controls seek via normal scroll, and focus entering a panel brings its scene into view. Cleanup restores styles, tab order, observers and timelines. No data edits, dependencies, assets, deployment or Phase 10.
